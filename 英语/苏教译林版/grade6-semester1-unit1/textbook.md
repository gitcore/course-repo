# 六年级第一单元 - The king's new clothes

学习一般过去时，讲述国王的新衣的故事。

## 课程信息

- **难度**: 初级
- **级别**: 1
- **主题**: The king's new clothes 国王的新衣
- **语法重点**: 一般过去时；there was/were；Long long ago

## 对话列表

1. Long long ago, there was a king.
2. He liked new clothes.
3. One day, two men visited the king.
4. "My king, we can make new clothes for you."
5. The king was happy.
6. The two men showed the king his new clothes.
7. "My king, please try on these magic clothes. Clever people can see them. Foolish people can't see them."
8. The king walked through the city in his new clothes.
9. There were a lot of people in the street.
10. They looked at the king and shouted, "What beautiful clothes!"
11. A little boy pointed at the king and laughed, "Ha! Ha! The king isn't wearing any clothes!"
12. Miss Fox and her students are playing a game. They are telling a story. Each student says one sentence. Miss Fox starts.
13. Miss Fox: Long long ago, there was a mountain.
14. Sam: There was a house on the mountain.
15. Willy: An old man and a little boy lived in the house.
16. Billy: The old man told the boy a story.
17. It is Bobby's turn. He is thinking hard.
18. Miss Fox: Bobby, what's next?
19. Bobby: Long long ago, there was ... a mountain.
20. Ha! Ha! We have to start the story again.

## 知识点

### 单元主题

The king's new clothes 国王的新衣

### 核心词汇

- long long ago 很久以前
- magic 有魔力的，神奇的
- clever 聪明的
- foolish 愚蠢的
- through 穿过
- laugh 笑，大笑
- wear 穿
- tell 讲，叙述
- each 每个
- say 说
- sentence 句子
- quick 迅速的，快的
- think 想，思考
- another 又一个
- next 下一个
- little 小的，年幼的
- turn 机会
- child 孩子
- turn into 变成
- hard 努力地，费劲地

### 重点句型

- Long long ago, there was a king.
- He liked new clothes.
- Two men visited the king.
- Clever people can see them.
- Foolish people can't see them.
- The king walked through the city in his new clothes.
- The king isn't wearing any clothes.

### 语法重点

一般过去时：
- Long long ago, there was a king.
- He liked new clothes.
- Two men visited the king.

there be 句型（过去时）：
- there was + 单数名词
- there were + 复数名词

# 五年级第六单元 - In the kitchen

学习厨房、食物、现在进行时一般疑问句和 there be 句型。

## 课程信息

- **难度**: 初级
- **级别**: 1
- **主题**: In the kitchen 在厨房
- **语法重点**: 现在进行时一般疑问句；there be 句型

## 对话列表

1. It is six o'clock in the evening. Liu Tao comes home.
2. His parents are cooking dinner in the kitchen.
3. Liu Tao: Are you cooking meat, Mum?
4. Mrs Liu: No, I'm not. I'm washing some vegetables.
5. Mrs Liu: I want to cook some tomato soup.
6. Mr Liu: I'm cooking meat with potatoes, Taotao.
7. Liu Tao: Great! I can't wait, Dad!
8. Liu Tao is looking for some juice in the fridge.
9. Liu Tao: Is there any apple juice in the fridge?
10. Mrs Liu: No, but there's some orange juice.
11. Dinner is ready. Liu Tao is eating the meat.
12. Mr Liu: How's the meat, Taotao?
13. Liu Tao: It's yummy, Dad. I love it! You're a great cook!
14. Mrs Liu: How's my soup, Taotao?
15. Liu Tao: It's nice, Mum. I love it too!

## 知识点

### 单元主题

In the kitchen 在厨房

### 核心词汇

- meat 肉
- potatoes 土豆
- tomatoes 西红柿
- vegetables 蔬菜
- tomato soup 西红柿汤
- kitchen 厨房
- fridge 冰箱
- juice 果汁
- ready 准备好的
- yummy 好吃的
- cook 厨师；烧，煮
- look for 寻找
- love 喜爱

### 重点句型

- Are you cooking meat?
- No, I'm not.
- I'm washing some vegetables.
- Is there any apple juice in the fridge?
- There's some orange juice.
- How's the meat?
- It's yummy.

### 语法重点

现在进行时一般疑问句：Be + 主语 + 动词 ing...?

- Are you cooking meat?
- Is Liu Tao looking for juice?

there be 句型表示“有”：

- Is there any apple juice in the fridge?
- There is some orange juice.

# 六年级第三单元 - Holiday fun

学习一般过去时，谈论假期活动和旅行经历。

## 课程信息

- **难度**: 初级
- **级别**: 1
- **主题**: Holiday fun 假日娱乐
- **语法重点**: 一般过去时；疑问句 Did you...?；Because引导的原因状语从句

## 对话列表

1. The students came back to school after the National Day holiday.
2. Mike: Hello, Liu Tao! Where did you go for the holiday? I called you, but you weren't at home.
3. Liu Tao: I went to Shanghai and visited my aunt.
4. Mike: What did you do there?
5. Liu Tao: We went to the Bund and visited the Shanghai Museum. I saw many interesting things.
6. Liu Tao: How was your holiday, Mike?
7. Mike: It was great fun. Our family went to a farm near Star Lake. We picked some oranges and went fishing.
8. Liu Tao: Did you catch any fish?
9. Mike: Yes, I did. I caught a big fish!
10. Liu Tao: That's great. Why did you call me?
11. Mike: Because I wanted to give you the fish.
12. Liu Tao: Where's the fish now?
13. Mike: I ate it!
14. It is time for dinner. Bobby comes home late.
15. Tina: Where did you go, Bobby?
16. Bobby: I was in the park. We had a fashion show there.
17. Tina loves beautiful clothes. She is excited about the show.
18. Tina: Wow, I love fashion shows! What did you wear, Bobby?
19. Bobby: I wore a paper T-shirt and paper shorts.
20. Tina: Ha! Ha! That's cool. What great fun!
21. Tina asks Bobby about Sam.
22. Tina: Did Sam wear paper clothes too?
23. Bobby: No. He wore a lot of bottles.
24. Tina: Wow, that's interesting!
25. Tina asks about the show.
26. Tina: Did the show go well?
27. Bobby: It was wonderful at first, but then there was heavy rain.
28. Tina: Oh, that's too bad!

## 知识点

### 单元主题

Holiday fun 假日娱乐

### 核心词汇

- holiday 假日，假期
- Bund 外滩
- Shanghai Museum 上海博物馆
- catch 抓住，捉住
- fish 鱼
- pick 摘
- orange 橘子
- bottle 瓶子
- fashion 时装
- paper 纸
- excited 激动的，兴奋的
- cool 很棒
- because 因为
- Great Wall 长城
- Palace Museum 故宫
- Summer Palace 颐和园
- Tian'anmen Square 天安门广场
- heavy rain 大雨

### 重点句型

- Where did you go for the holiday?
- I went to Shanghai and visited my aunt.
- We went to the Bund and visited the Shanghai Museum.
- How was your holiday, Mike?
- It was great fun.
- Did you catch any fish?
- Yes, I did. I caught a big fish!
- Because I wanted to give you the fish.
- We had a fashion show there.
- What did you wear, Bobby?
- He wore a lot of bottles.
- Did the show go well?

### 语法重点

一般过去时（疑问句）：
- Where did you go...?
- What did you do...?
- Did you catch any fish?
- Yes, I did. / No, I didn't.

Because 引导原因状语从句：
- Because I wanted to give you the fish.

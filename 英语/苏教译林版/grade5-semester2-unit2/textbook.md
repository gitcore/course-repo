# 五年级第二单元 - How do you come to school

学习关于家、居住和上学方式的表达

## 课程信息

- **难度**: 初级
- **级别**: 1

## Story time

1. Yang Ling: Do you like your new home, Su Hai?
2. Su Hai: Yes! It's very big. I like it very much, but it's far from school. Where do you live now?
3. Yang Ling: Where do you live now?
4. Su Hai: I live on Moon Street, near City Library.
5. Yang Ling: How do you come to school?
6. Su Hai: Su Yang and I come to school by bus.
7. Mike: Where do you live, Yang Ling? How do you come to school?
8. Yang Ling: I live near school. I come to school on foot. Where do you live?
9. Mike: I live in Sunshine Town. I come to school by metro. What about you, Liu Tao?
10. Liu Tao: I live on Park Street. I come to school by taxi. My father is a taxi driver.

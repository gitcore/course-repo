# 六年级第二单元 - What a day!

学习一般过去时，描述一天的经历和天气变化。

## 课程信息

- **难度**: 初级
- **级别**: 1
- **主题**: What a day! 多糟糕的一天
- **语法重点**: 一般过去时；天气描述；感叹句 What a day!

## 对话列表

1. Sunday, 20th September
2. It was sunny in the morning. Su Hai, Mike, Liu Tao and I went to the park by bike.
3. There was a parrot show in the park. We saw some interesting parrots.
4. Then, the weather became windy and cloudy. We flew kites high in the sky.
5. It was time for lunch. We brought some jiaozi, some bread and honey and some drinks.
6. We saw some ants on the bread and honey. There were some bees too. We could not eat our lunch!
7. In the afternoon, there were black clouds in the sky. It rained. We were hungry and wet.
8. What a day!
9. Sam meets Bobby in the park.
10. Sam: Hi, Bobby. You look sad. What's the matter?
11. Bobby: I lost my new kite.
12. Sam wants to know why.
13. Sam: What happened, Bobby?
14. Bobby: This morning, Tina and I flew my new kite in the park, but it wasn't windy in the park.
15. Sam: What happened then?
16. Bobby: We climbed up the hill. It was windy there.
17. Sam: What happened then?
18. Bobby: The kite flew high, but it flew too high and we couldn't hold onto it. It flew away.
19. Sam laughs.
20. Sam: Is this your kite?
21. Bobby: Yes!
22. Sam: Why do you have it?
23. Sam: I found it near the hill!

## 知识点

### 单元主题

What a day! 多糟糕的一天

### 核心词汇

- sunny 晴朗的
- show 展览，展示
- interesting 有趣的，有意思的
- weather 天气
- become 变成，变为
- windy 有风的
- cloudy 多云的
- high 在高处
- sky 天空
- bring 带来
- honey 蜂蜜
- drink 饮料
- ant 蚂蚁
- bee 蜜蜂
- cloud 云
- rain 雨
- rainy 多雨的
- meet 遇见
- lose 丢失
- know 知道

### 重点句型

- It was sunny in the morning.
- We went to the park by bike.
- There was a parrot show in the park.
- The weather became windy and cloudy.
- We flew kites high in the sky.
- We brought some jiaozi, some bread and honey and some drinks.
- We could not eat our lunch!
- What a day!
- I lost my new kite.
- The kite flew away.

### 语法重点

一般过去时（动词过去式）：
- is/am -> was
- go -> went
- see -> saw
- become -> became
- fly -> flew
- bring -> brought
- can -> could
- rain -> rained
- are -> were
- lose -> lost
- climb -> climbed
- find -> found

感叹句：
- What a day! 多糟糕的一天！
- What + a/an + 名词 + (主语 + 谓语)!

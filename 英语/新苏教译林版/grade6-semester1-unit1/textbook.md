# 六年级上册第一单元 - Try your best

学习一般过去时，讲述苏海参演校园话剧《花木兰》、尽力而为的故事。

## 课程信息

- **难度**: 初级
- **级别**: 1
- **主题**: Try your best 尽你最大的努力
- **语法重点**: 一般过去时；be动词过去式 was/were；规则动词ed；不规则动词过去式

## 对话列表

1. Two weeks ago, Su Hai wanted to play Mulan in a school play.
2. It was her first school play.
3. She wanted to try her best.
4. There were a lot of words in the play.
5. She asked Su Yang to practise with her.
6. There were also a lot of movements.
7. She used her dancing skills to do them well.
8. Su Hai was afraid of acting in front of many people.
9. She asked her family to watch her practise.
10. Su Hai practised a lot.
11. She remembered all the words and movements.
12. She was ready.
13. Finally, it was time for the play.
14. Su Hai acted well. It was great!
15. Last week, Bobby and Sam saw a new poster at school.
16. Bobby: I'm not good at singing.
17. Sam: But you like singing! Have a go!
18. Bobby practised in the morning, in the afternoon and in the evening.
19. First he was afraid, but then he calmed down.
20. First he forgot the words, but then he remembered them.
21. Everyone: You were great, Bobby!
22. Bobby: I want to sing again next year!

## 知识点

### 单元主题

Try your best 尽你最大的努力

### 核心词汇

- try one's best 尽某人最大的努力
- act 扮演，演出
- play 戏剧；扮演
- poster 海报，招贴画
- last 上一个的，最近的
- take part 参与
- practise 练习，训练
- afraid 害怕，畏惧
- forget 忘记，遗忘
- remember 记起；记住
- begin 开始
- words 台词
- movement 动作
- skill 技能，技术
- ready 准备好的
- finally 最后，终于
- great 极好的；伟大的
- calm 镇静的
- excited 激动的，兴奋的
- ago 以前
- give it a go 试一试，试试看

### 重点句型

- Two weeks ago, Su Hai wanted to play Mulan in a school play.
- It was her first school play.
- She wanted to try her best.
- There were a lot of words in the play.
- She asked Su Yang to practise with her.
- Su Hai was afraid of acting in front of many people.
- Finally, it was time for the play.
- I'm not good at singing.
- Have a go!
- First he was afraid, but then he calmed down.

### 语法重点

一般过去时：
- be动词过去式：was (单数) / were (复数)
- 规则动词过去式：动词 + ed (wanted, asked, practised, used)
- 不规则动词过去式：see → saw, forget → forgot, begin → began

there be 句型（过去时）：
- there was + 单数名词
- there were + 复数名词

# Unit 8 Then and now — 课文与知识点

## 单元概览

**单元主题：** 过去与现在，科技与生活变化。  
**Big question：** How does technology change your life?（科技怎样改变你的生活？）  
**Cartoon time：** A "smart" robot（一个“聪明的”机器人）。  
**Story time：** Changes in life（生活的变化）：王兵采访爷爷奶奶、妈妈和爸爸。  
**语法：** I do ... I did ... (review)，比较现在常做的事与过去做过的事。  
**语音：** Sounds in focus，按意群停顿。

## 对话列表

### Cartoon time — A "smart" robot

1. Two weeks ago, Bobby's parents bought a robot from a shop.
2. Bobby's father: Look at this robot! He's very smart!
3. Bobby's mother: He can do many things!
4. Bobby: Cool!
5. The robot cooked a big dinner. Bobby, Tina and their parents liked the food very much.
6. Tina: It's delicious!
7. Bobby: What a smart robot!
8. The robot played Chinese chess with Bobby. He won every time.
9. Bobby: How smart!
10. One day, Bobby, Tina and their parents went to the park. They came back late.
11. Bobby: I'm hungry. What's for dinner today?
12. Tina: Our robot is cooking dinner for us. He'll give us a surprise.
13. Bobby: I can't wait!
14. Bobby's mother: What happened?
15. Bobby: Where's our dinner?
16. Tina: Where's the robot?
17. Tina: Look! Here's our robot!
18. Bobby: Oh no! What's wrong with the robot?
### Sounds in focus — Listen and read along

19. Last week, I bought a robot online.
20. It does a lot of chores.
21. It never gets tired.
22. I can talk to it on the screen.
23. It makes my house clean.
### Story time — Changes in life

24. Wang Bing: Hi, Grandpa and Grandma. I'm doing a project on life in the past and now. Can you tell me about the changes in your life?
25. Grandpa: Sure. In the past, I wrote letters to my family and friends. Now I chat with them on the internet.
26. Grandma: And we spent a lot of time on the road then. Now high-speed trains travel very fast.
27. Wang Bing: Cool!
28. Wang Bing: Mum, what was your life like in the past?
29. Mum: In the past, I worked in an office. These days, I sometimes work from home and have online meetings.
30. Wang Bing: What about you, Dad?
31. Dad: Twenty years ago, I went to shops and paid in cash. Now I often shop online and pay on my smartphone.
32. Dad: Oh no!
33. Wang Bing: Don't worry, Dad. We have a robot helper.

## 配套知识（非新增教材对白）

### 过去和现在的对比

- 爷爷：wrote letters → chat on the internet。
- 奶奶：spent a lot of time on the road → high-speed trains travel very fast。
- 妈妈：worked in an office → sometimes work from home and have online meetings。
- 爸爸：went to shops and paid in cash → often shop online and pay on my smartphone。

这些是课文人物的经历，不等于所有人过去或现在都用同一种方式生活。新版核心语法是一般过去时与一般现在时的复习；不要把旧版Mike的could/couldn't段落当成本课原文。

### 重点词语

| 英语 | 中文 |
|---|---|
| technology | 科技；技术 |
| smart / smartphone | 聪明的、智能的 / 智能手机 |
| Chinese chess | 中国象棋（不是国际象棋） |
| surprise | 惊喜；意外 |
| chores | 家务活 |
| screen | 屏幕 |
| in the past / these days | 在过去 / 如今 |
| chat on the internet | 在网上聊天 |
| high-speed train | 高速列车；高铁 |
| work from home | 居家工作 |
| online meeting | 线上会议 |
| pay in cash | 用现金付款 |
| robot helper | 机器人助手 |

### 动词与时态

不规则过去式：buy–bought、win–won、go–went、come–came、write–wrote、spend–spent、pay–paid。规则过去式：cook–cooked、like–liked、play–played、work–worked。ago和in the past在本课中提示过去情境；now和these days提示现在的习惯或状态。He'll是He will的缩写，表示将来；is cooking表示正在做饭。

### 按意群朗读

教材标记（/为停顿提示，不是要读出的词）：

- Two weeks ago, / Bobby's parents / bought a robot / from a shop.
- Our robot / is cooking dinner / for us.
- Last week, / I bought a robot / online.
- It does / a lot of chores.
- It never / gets tired.
- I can talk to it / on the screen.
- It makes / my house / clean.

停顿随句意和语速调整，不是每个主语、介词或连词前后都必须停顿。

### 阅读与讨论边界

机器人故事结尾提出What's wrong with the robot?，没有用对白说明它没电、彻底损坏或已经修好。可以依据图片讨论可能原因，但要明确这是推测。Sounds in focus中的It never gets tired是练习文本中的表达，不应扩展为所有现实机器人无需电源、维护或休息的技术结论。

### 配套写作练习（原创）

采访家人，记录过去和现在的一项生活变化。可用In the past, I ... / Now I ...，区分事实与自己的评价，不必把每种变化都写成“越来越好”。例如：In the past, I wrote letters. Now I chat with my friends online.（配套示例，不是额外课文段落。）

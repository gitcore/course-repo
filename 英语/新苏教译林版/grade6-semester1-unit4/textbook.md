# Unit 4 Getting together — 课文与知识点

## 单元概览

**单元主题：** 聚在一起  
**核心问题：** How can you make your parents and grandparents happy?（怎样让爸爸妈妈和祖父母开心？）  
**核心故事：**
- **Cartoon time:** Happy Double Ninth Festival — Bobby和Tina陪爷爷过重阳节
- **Story time:** Grandma's birthday — 王兵一家为奶奶过66岁生日

**语法重点：**
- 序数词：21st, 66th, 9th 等
- 日期表达：21st October
- 一般过去时叙述事件
- 节日名称表达
- 字母组合 er 的发音 /ə/

## 对话列表

### Cartoon time — Happy Double Ninth Festival

1. It is the Double Ninth Festival today.
2. Bobby: Do you know the Double Ninth Festival, Tina?
3. Tina: Yes, it's a day for old people. It's on the ninth day of the ninth lunar month.
4. Bobby: What do people do on this day?
5. Tina: They show their love for their grandmothers and grandfathers. They give them gifts and do chores for them.
6. Bobby: What should we do for Grandpa?
7. Tina: We can cook dinner for him.
8. Bobby: Good idea! We can make a card for him too.
9. Tina: Great!
10. Bobby, Tina and their parents go to see Grandpa in the afternoon.
11. Bobby & Tina: Happy Double Ninth Festival, Grandpa! We love you!
12. Grandpa: Thank you! I love you too!
13. Bobby's father: My birthday is next Monday. I can't wait!

### Sounds in focus — 字母组合 er 的发音 /ə/

15. And put them in some water.
16. I made her a card and wrote on it:
17. "With love, from your daughter".

### Story time — Grandma's birthday

18. Wang Bing: Yesterday was 21st October.
19. Wang Bing: It was Grandma's 66th birthday.
20. Wang Bing: We went to my grandparents' house in the morning.
21. Wang Bing: My parents gave Grandma a nice smartphone.
22. Wang Bing: I drew a picture for her.
23. Wang Bing: We also brought a cake.
24. Wang Bing: Grandpa and Grandma were so happy to see us.
25. Wang Bing: We had a big lunch.
26. Wang Bing: We ate noodles and wished Grandma a long life.
27. Wang Bing: Then Grandma blew out the candles, and we ate the cake.
28. Wang Bing: In the afternoon, I chatted with Grandma.
29. Wang Bing: We also took a family photo together.
30. Wang Bing: We do this on Grandma's birthday every year.
31. Wang Bing: This is our 12th photo.
32. Wang Bing: Time flies!
33. Wang Bing: When I was a little kid, Grandpa and Grandma took care of me.
34. Wang Bing: Now I am a big boy.
35. Wang Bing: I can take care of them.
36. Wang Bing: I want to spend more time with them.

## 原页与角色说明

- 原页顺序：Cartoon time（第46–47页）→ Sounds in focus（第48页）→ Story time（第49–50页）。编号是本课程的朗读条目号；对白按气泡合并，歌谣按行，连续短文按句拆分。
- Cartoon time标题为Happy Double Ninth Festival。第9条“Great!”属于Tina。第13条“My birthday is next Monday. I can't wait!”是爸爸的同一个内心活动气泡，气泡指向站在右侧、穿橙色衣服的爸爸，不是爷爷或Bobby；读本标为Bobby's father。
- 第11条共同祝福的气泡指向Bobby与Tina；角色名根据插图补记，不属于印刷正文的一部分。
- Story time是王兵用I、my、we、our讲述的第一人称短文。依据插图与页尾“What did Wang Bing do to make his grandma happy?”确认叙述者，补记为Wang Bing，不把人称改写为he或they。
- 恢复每年在奶奶生日拍全家福、“This is our 12th photo.”与原有“Time flies!”位置；删除无原页依据的“We should always love our family.”，不把故事概括混成教材原句。
- Sounds in focus是给妈妈买花、制作卡片的歌谣，不是旧源中“My dear grandmother…”四行。
- 下方知识点、例句与写作模板为配套学习材料，并非Grammar time、Wrap-up time或词汇表的逐字抄录。

### 语法重点

#### 1. 序数词

序数词表示顺序，如「第几个」。常见变化规则：

- 一般在基数词后加 -th：four → fourth, six → sixth
- 特殊变化：one → first (1st), two → second (2nd), three → third (3rd)
- 以 ty 结尾的整十数，变 y 为 ie 再加 th：twenty → twentieth
- 几十几的数，只变个位：twenty-one → twenty-first (21st)

**课文序数词与配套拓展：**
- 21st (twenty-first) 第二十一
- 66th (sixty-sixth) 第六十六
- 9th (ninth) 第九
- 12th (twelfth) 第十二
- 1st (first) 第一
- 2nd (second) 第二
- 3rd (third) 第三

**常见序数词：**
| 基数词 | 序数词 | 缩写 |
|--------|--------|------|
| one | first | 1st |
| two | second | 2nd |
| three | third | 3rd |
| four | fourth | 4th |
| five | fifth | 5th |
| six | sixth | 6th |
| seven | seventh | 7th |
| eight | eighth | 8th |
| nine | ninth | 9th |
| ten | tenth | 10th |
| twelve | twelfth | 12th |

#### 2. 日期表达

英式英语日期格式：**序数词 + 月份**
- 21st October = 10月21日
- 读作：the twenty-first of October

- October 21st = 10月21日

#### 3. 一般过去时回顾

本单元继续巩固一般过去时，叙述过去发生的生日活动：

- went → go（去）
- gave → give（给）
- drew → draw（画）
- brought → bring（带来）
- ate → eat（吃）
- blew → blow（吹）
- chatted → chat（聊天）
- took → take（拿/拍）
- wished → wish（祝愿）
- was/were → be（是）

#### 4. 双宾语结构

give sb. sth. = give sth. to sb. 给某人某物
- They gave Grandma a smartphone. = They gave a smartphone to Grandma.

类似的动词还有：send, show, bring, tell 等。
- show sb. sth. = show sth. to sb.
- send sb. sth. = send sth. to sb.
- bring sb. sth. = bring sth. to sb.

#### 5. take care of

take care of 表示「照顾；照料」，同义词组是 look after。

- Grandpa and Grandma **took care of** me when I was little.
  我小的时候爷爷奶奶照顾我。
- Now I can **take care of** them.
  现在我可以照顾他们了。
- Please **take care of** your little sister.
  请照顾好你的小妹妹。

### 写作指导：写一次家庭聚会的经历

**写作任务：** Write about a family gathering you had.

**写作结构：**
1. **开头：** 什么时候？什么场合？和谁在一起？
   - Last Sunday was my grandma's birthday.
   - I went to my grandparents' house with my parents.
2. **经过：** 你们做了什么？（用一般过去时）
   - We gave her gifts.
   - We had a big dinner.
   - We took a family photo.
3. **结尾：** 你的感受如何？
   - We had a good time.
   - I love my family.
   - Family is important.

**常用表达：**
- have a big dinner 吃一顿丰盛的晚餐
- give gifts 送礼物
- make a cake 做蛋糕
- sing birthday songs 唱生日歌
- take photos 拍照
- have a good time 玩得开心
- chat with 和……聊天
- help with 帮忙做……

**写作模板：**
```
Last _________, I went to _________ with _________.
It was _________ birthday.
We _________.
I gave _________ to _________.
We _________ together.
We took a family photo.
I had a good time.
I love my family.
```

### 故事寓意

**Cartoon time：** 重阳节是敬老爱老的节日。尊敬老人、孝敬长辈是中华民族的传统美德。我们可以用实际行动来表达对爷爷奶奶的爱。

**Story time：** 爷爷奶奶照顾我们长大，我们也要学会关心他们。陪伴是最长情的告白，多花时间和家人在一起就是最好的爱。

**Family is the most important thing in the world. 家人是世界上最重要的。**

### 文化拓展

#### 重阳节

重阳节，又称「老人节」，在农历九月初九。「九」在中国文化中是吉祥的数字，九九重阳寓意长久长寿。重阳节的习俗包括：
- 登高赏菊
- 吃重阳糕
- 孝敬老人，表达对长辈的关爱

#### 生日长寿面

中国有生日吃面条的习俗，长长的面条象征「长寿」，所以叫「长寿面」。这是对寿星的美好祝愿。

#### 中国传统节日

- **春节 Spring Festival**：农历正月初一，最隆重的节日
- **端午节 Dragon Boat Festival**：农历五月初五，纪念屈原
- **中秋节 Mid-Autumn Festival**：农历八月十五，赏月吃月饼
- **重阳节 Double Ninth Festival**：农历九月初九，敬老爱老

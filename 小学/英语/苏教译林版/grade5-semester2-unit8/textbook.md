# 五年级第八单元 - Birthdays

学习询问生日、月份与序数词、描述生日活动。

## 课程信息

- **难度**: 初级
- **级别**: 1
- **主题**: Birthdays 生日
- **语法重点**: 序数词；询问生日 When's your birthday?

## 对话列表

1. Miss Li: When's your birthday, Su Hai?
2. Su Hai: It's on the eleventh of May. It's also Su Yang's birthday.
3. Miss Li: What do you do on your birthday?
4. Su Hai: We usually have a big dinner with our parents and grandparents. Su Yang and I eat some noodles. After that, we play with Kitty the cat. We have a great time.
5. Miss Li: When's your birthday, Mike?
6. Mike: It's on the eighth of April. We always have a party at home. Helen, Tim and I go to buy a birthday cake in the morning. Then, my friends come in the afternoon. We eat the birthday cake together and play some games. We have a lot of fun!
7. Bobby is a hero in a play.
8. Help! Help!
9. How can I open the door?
10. Bobby sees some numbers on the door.
11. Is this the password? That's easy for me. I'm good at Maths.
12. Bobby has the answers.
13. The first number is six. The second number is three. The third number is also six. The fourth number is two.
14. The door opens. Bobby goes in. There is a pig in the house. They start fighting.
15. The pig runs away. Then, a cat comes out from a room.
16. Thank you so much!
17. Oh, what a play!

## 知识点

### 单元主题

Birthdays 生日

### 核心词汇

- birthday 生日
- eleventh 第十一
- eighth 第八
- fourth 第四
- April 四月
- May 五月
- March 三月
- July 七月
- August 八月
- December 十二月
- together 一起
- game 游戏
- hero 英雄
- play 戏剧
- number 数字
- password 密码
- answer 答案
- start 开始
- fight 打架
- go shopping 去购物

### 重点句型

- When's your birthday?
- It's on the eleventh of May.
- It's on the eighth of April.
- What do you do on your birthday?
- We usually have a big dinner with our parents and grandparents.
- Su Yang and I eat some noodles.
- We play with Kitty the cat.
- We have a party at home.
- go to buy a birthday cake
- We eat the birthday cake together and play some games.

### 语法重点

询问生日的句型：
- When is your birthday?
- It's on + 序数词 + of + 月份

序数词的构成：
- first, second, third（特殊）
- fourth, fifth, sixth...（基数词 + th）
- eighth（特殊）
- ninth（去e）
- twelfth（特殊）
- 21st, 22nd, 23rd 等

询问生日活动的句型：
- What do you do on your birthday?
- I/We usually + 动词短语

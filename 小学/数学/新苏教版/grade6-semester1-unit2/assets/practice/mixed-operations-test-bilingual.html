中文

  1. 先算除法：3/4 ÷ 9/8 = 2/3；再算 5/6 − 2/3 = 1/6。
  2. 5/6 + 1/4 = 13/12；13/12 ÷ 1/2 = 13/6；2.4 × 13/6 = 5.2。不能把 5/6 截成近似小数后当作准确值。
  3. 1.25 × (7/9 + 2/9) = 1.25；使用乘法分配律。
  4. 18.6 < 19，21.7 < 22，实际费用 < 3 × 19 + 2 × 22 = 101 元；101 元够。
  5. 24.8 > 24，14.9 > 14，实际费用 > 3 × 24 + 2 × 14 = 100 元；100 元不够。
  6. 单位“1”是整卷布；剩下 120 × (1 − 3/8) = 75 米。
  7. 单位“1”是去年种的 96 棵；今年 96 × (1 + 5/12) = 136 棵。
  8. 科技类 90 本是单位“1”；故事类 90 × (1 − 2/5) = 54 本，共 90 + 54 = 144 本。
  9. 工作总量看作 1；甲每小时做 1/5，乙每小时做 1/10；合作时间 1 ÷ (1/5 + 1/10) = 10/3 小时，即 3 小时 20 分。
  10. 甲先做 2 × 1/6 = 1/3，剩 2/3；合作效率 1/6 + 1/12 = 1/4，合作 2/3 ÷ 1/4 = 8/3 小时；总时间 2 + 8/3 = 14/3 小时，即 4 小时 40 分。

English

  1. Divide first: 3/4 ÷ 9/8 = 2/3. Then 5/6 − 2/3 = 1/6.
  2. 5/6 + 1/4 = 13/12; 13/12 ÷ 1/2 = 13/6; 2.4 × 13/6 = 5.2. Do not use a truncated decimal as the exact value of 5/6.
  3. 1.25 × (7/9 + 2/9) = 1.25, by the distributive property.
  4. 18.6 < 19 and 21.7 < 22, so the actual cost is < 3 × 19 + 2 × 22 = ¥101. The budget is enough.
  5. 24.8 > 24 and 14.9 > 14, so the actual cost is > 3 × 24 + 2 × 14 = ¥100. The budget is not enough.
  6. The whole is the 120 m roll; 120 × (1 − 3/8) = 75 m remain.
  7. Last year's 96 trees are the base amount; 96 × (1 + 5/12) = 136 trees this year.
  8. The 90 science books are the base amount; 90 × (1 − 2/5) = 54 storybooks, and 90 + 54 = 144 books in all.
  9. Let the whole job be 1. A's rate is 1/5 per hour and B's is 1/10; 1 ÷ (1/5 + 1/10) = 10/3 h = 3 h 20 min.
  10. A first finishes 2 × 1/6 = 1/3, leaving 2/3. Together their rate is 1/6 + 1/12 = 1/4. The joint stage takes 8/3 h; total 2 + 8/3 = 14/3 h = 4 h 40 min.

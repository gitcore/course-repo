中文

  1. 4 表示 4 个百，3 表示 3 个十分之一，5 表示 5 个百分之一。
  2. 分数单位是 1/8，有 5 个这样的单位；5/8 = 0.625。
  3. 0.58 < 0.6 = 3/5。
  4. 0.7 = 70/100，3/20 = 15/100；70/100 + 15/100 = 85/100 = 0.85。
  5. □ = 2.4 − 1.35 = 1.05；验算：1.05 + 1.35 = 2.4。
  6. 3/4 × 2/3 = 1/2 米；2/3 表示剪去部分占原绳长的 2/3。
  7. 3 个 0.1 与 4 个 0.01 相乘得到 12 个 0.001，所以 0.3 × 0.04 = 0.012。
  8. 16 × (3/4 + 1/4) = 16 × 1 = 16；使用乘法分配律。
  9. □ = 2.4 ÷ 0.8 = 3；验算：0.8 × 3 = 2.4。
  10. 3/4 ÷ 3/8 = 3/4 × 8/3 = 2。0 没有倒数；用乘法逆运算理解，除以 0 无法确定唯一的商。

English

  1. 4 means four hundreds; 3 means three tenths; 5 means five hundredths.
  2. The unit fraction is 1/8; there are five such units; 5/8 = 0.625.
  3. 0.58 < 0.6 = 3/5.
  4. 0.7 = 70/100 and 3/20 = 15/100; their sum is 85/100 = 0.85.
  5. □ = 2.4 − 1.35 = 1.05; check: 1.05 + 1.35 = 2.4.
  6. 3/4 × 2/3 = 1/2 m. The fraction 2/3 is the part of the original rope cut off.
  7. Three tenths times four hundredths is twelve thousandths: 0.3 × 0.04 = 0.012.
  8. 16 × (3/4 + 1/4) = 16 × 1 = 16, using the distributive property.
  9. □ = 2.4 ÷ 0.8 = 3; check: 0.8 × 3 = 2.4.
  10. 3/4 ÷ 3/8 = 3/4 × 8/3 = 2. Zero has no reciprocal; division by zero cannot give a unique quotient.

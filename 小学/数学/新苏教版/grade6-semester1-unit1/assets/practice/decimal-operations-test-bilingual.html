中文

  1. 18 × 0.35 = 6.3（升）；0.35 表示水箱容量的百分之三十五。
  2. 错。8 × 7 = 56，两因数共三位小数，正确的积是 0.056。
  3. 依次填 ＞、＜、＜、＞。正数乘大于 1 的数变大，乘 0 到 1 之间的数变小；除法则相反。
  4. 4.8 × 0.25 = 1.2：一个因数乘 10、另一个除以 10，积不变；0.48 × 0.25 = 0.12：第二个因数除以 10，积也除以 10。
  5. 2.75 × 1.34 = 3.685 ≈ 3.69（保留两位小数）。
  6. 8.64 ÷ 0.16 = 864 ÷ 16 = 54；被除数和除数同时乘 100，商不变。
  7. 只把除数乘 100，没有把被除数也乘 100。正确转化为 72 ÷ 6 = 12。
  8. 7.35 ÷ 0.6 = 73.5 ÷ 6 = 12.25；验算：12.25 × 0.6 = 7.35。
  9. 5.2 ÷ 0.9 = 5.777… ≈ 5.78；至少算到第三位，即千分位。
  10. 9 × 2.6 = 23.4，10 × 2.6 = 26，所以最多装满 9 袋；剩下 0.4 千克仍需一袋，全部装完至少要 10 袋。

English

  1. 18 × 0.35 = 6.3 L; 0.35 means 35% of the tank's capacity.
  2. No. 8 × 7 = 56, and the factors have three decimal places in total, so the product is 0.056.
  3. The signs are >, <, <, >. Multiplying a positive number by a factor above 1 makes it larger; a factor between 0 and 1 makes it smaller. Division reverses these comparisons.
  4. 4.8 × 0.25 = 1.2: one factor is multiplied by 10 and the other divided by 10. 0.48 × 0.25 = 0.12: dividing one factor by 10 divides the product by 10.
  5. 2.75 × 1.34 = 3.685 ≈ 3.69 to two decimal places.
  6. 8.64 ÷ 0.16 = 864 ÷ 16 = 54; multiplying both dividend and divisor by 100 leaves the quotient unchanged.
  7. The student multiplied only the divisor by 100. Multiply both numbers by 100: 72 ÷ 6 = 12.
  8. 7.35 ÷ 0.6 = 73.5 ÷ 6 = 12.25; check: 12.25 × 0.6 = 7.35.
  9. 5.2 ÷ 0.9 = 5.777… ≈ 5.78; calculate through the thousandths place first.
  10. 9 × 2.6 = 23.4 and 10 × 2.6 = 26. You can fill 9 bags completely; the remaining 0.4 kg needs another bag, so at least 10 bags are needed for all the food.

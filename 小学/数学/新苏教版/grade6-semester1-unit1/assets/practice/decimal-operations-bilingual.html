参考答案 / Answer key

中文

  1. 6 × 0.3 = 1.8（元）；0.3 表示 1 千克的十分之三。
  2. 6.4 × 0.8 < 6.4；6.4 ÷ 0.8 > 6.4，因为 0 < 0.8 < 1。
  3. 24 × 35 = 840；两个因数共四位小数，0.24 × 0.35 = 0.0840 = 0.084。
  4. 3.75 ÷ 0.15 = 375 ÷ 15 = 25；两个数同时扩大 100 倍。
  5. 2.52 ÷ 0.6 = 25.2 ÷ 6 = 4.2；验算：4.2 × 0.6 = 2.52。
  6. 4.85 × 1.6 = 7.76 ≈ 7.8（保留一位小数）。
  7. 2.8 ÷ 0.6 = 4.666… ≈ 4.67；至少算到千分位。
  8. 13.6 ÷ 2.5 = 5.44；5 个袋子装不完，至少要 6 个。
  9. 13.6 ÷ 2.5 = 5.44；只能做 5 个完整的蝴蝶结。
  10. 2.6 × 0.14 = 0.364：第二个因数除以 10，积也除以 10。0.26 × 14 = 3.64：一个因数除以 10，另一个因数乘 10，积不变。

English

  1. 6 × 0.3 = ¥1.8; 0.3 means three tenths of a kilogram.
  2. 6.4 × 0.8 < 6.4; 6.4 ÷ 0.8 > 6.4, because 0 < 0.8 < 1.
  3. 24 × 35 = 840; there are four decimal places in total, so 0.24 × 0.35 = 0.084.
  4. 3.75 ÷ 0.15 = 375 ÷ 15 = 25; multiply both numbers by 100.
  5. 2.52 ÷ 0.6 = 25.2 ÷ 6 = 4.2; check: 4.2 × 0.6 = 2.52.
  6. 4.85 × 1.6 = 7.76 ≈ 7.8 (one decimal place).
  7. 2.8 ÷ 0.6 = 4.666… ≈ 4.67; calculate through the thousandths place.
  8. 13.6 ÷ 2.5 = 5.44; five bags are not enough, so six bags are needed.
  9. 13.6 ÷ 2.5 = 5.44; only five complete bows can be made.
  10. 2.6 × 0.14 = 0.364: dividing one factor by 10 divides the product by 10. 0.26 × 14 = 3.64: dividing one factor by 10 and multiplying the other by 10 leaves the product unchanged.

# 五年级第四单元 - Seeing the doctor

学习看病相关的表达和短语

## 课程信息

- **难度**: 初级
- **级别**: 1

## Story time

1. Su Hai is ill. She goes to see the doctor.
2. Doctor: What's wrong with you?
3. Su Hai: I have a headache. I feel cold.
4. Doctor: Let me check. You have a fever.
5. Su Hai: What should I do, Doctor?
6. Doctor: You should have a rest at home. You should take some medicine and drink some warm water.
7. Su Hai: Thank you, Doctor.
8. Mike has a toothache. He goes to see the dentist.
9. Dentist: What's wrong with you?
10. Mike: I have a toothache. I can't eat anything!
11. Dentist: Do you eat a lot of sweets?
12. Mike: Yes, I do.
13. Dentist: You shouldn't eat too many sweets. You should brush your teeth in the morning and before bedtime.
14. Mike: Ok, Thanks.

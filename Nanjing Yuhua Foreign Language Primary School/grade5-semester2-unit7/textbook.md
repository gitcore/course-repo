# 五年级第七单元 - Chinese festivals

学习中国传统节日、月份表达和节日活动描述。

## 课程信息

- **难度**: 初级
- **级别**: 1
- **主题**: Chinese festivals 中国节日
- **语法重点**: 一般现在时描述节日；in + 月份表达

## 对话列表

1. The Spring Festival is in January or February. People also call it Chinese New Year. At this festival, people get together with their families. Some people eat dumplings.
2. The Dragon Boat Festival is in May or June. There are dragon boat races in some places. People eat rice dumplings at this festival.
3. The Mid-Autumn Festival is in September or October. People look at the moon at night with their families. They eat moon cakes and fruit.
4. The Double Ninth Festival is in October or November. It is a festival for old people. People visit their parents and grandparents. They also climb mountains and eat rice cakes at this festival.
5. It is Mother's Day today.
6. Tina: Do you know Mother's Day, Bobby?
7. Bobby: No, I don't. What is it?
8. Tina: It's a day for mothers. It's on the second Sunday of May.
9. Bobby likes all festivals.
10. Bobby: What do people do on Mother's Day?
11. Tina: They give their mothers presents.
12. Bobby and Tina talk about the present for Mum.
13. Bobby: What should we give Mum?
14. Tina: What about a card and some flowers?
15. Bobby: That's a good idea!
16. Mum and Dad come home.
17. Tina and Bobby: Happy Mother's Day, Mum! We love you!
18. Mum: Thank you! I love you too!
19. Dad: Is there a Father's Day too?

### 语法重点

一般现在时描述节日时间：节日名称 + is in + 月份

- The Spring Festival is in January or February.
- The Dragon Boat Festival is in May or June.

描述节日活动：People + 动词短语 + at this festival

- People eat rice dumplings at this festival.
- People climb mountains and eat rice cakes at this festival.

in 和 on 的区别：

- in + 月份：in January, in May
- on + 具体某一天：on the second Sunday of May

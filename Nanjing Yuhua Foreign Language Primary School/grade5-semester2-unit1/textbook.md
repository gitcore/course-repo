# 五年级第一单元 - Cinderella

学习关于灰姑娘故事的表达

## 课程信息

- **难度**: 初级
- **级别**: 1

## Story time

1. There is a party at the prince's house, but Cinderella cannot go.
2. Sister: Cinderella, come and help me!
3. Sister: Cinderella, where are my gloves?
4. A fairy comes.
5. Fairy: Why are you so sad, dear?
6. Cinderella: Because I can't go to the party.
7. Fairy: Why?
8. Cinderella: Because I don't have any nice clothes or shoes.
9. Fairy: Let me help you.
10. Cinderella puts on the new clothes and shoes.
11. Fairy: Come back before 12 o'clock.
12. Cinderella has a good time at the party.
13. Cinderella: Sorry, I have to go now.
14. Prince: Hey, your shoe!
15. The prince visits every house. Many girls try on the shoe, but it does not fit. Finally, Cinderella tries it on.
16. Prince: It fits!

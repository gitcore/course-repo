# 五年级第五单元 - Helping our parents

学习帮助父母、家务劳动和现在进行时的表达。

## 课程信息

- **难度**: 初级
- **级别**: 1
- **主题**: Helping our parents 帮助父母
- **语法重点**: 现在进行时 be + 动词 ing

## 对话列表

1. It is Saturday morning. My father is cleaning the car. I am helping him.
2. My mother is cooking breakfast in the kitchen. Helen is in the living room. She is sweeping the floor.
3. Tim is in his bedroom. He is sleeping. Ben the dog is sleeping too.
4. Where is Mike?
5. Mike is in the garden. He is washing the dishes. What about Tim and Ben?
6. Tim and Ben are watching TV in the living room.
7. What are you doing, Dad?

9. What are you doing, Mum?
10. Mum: I am cooking breakfast.
11. What are you doing, Helen?
12. Helen: I am sweeping the floor.
13. What are you doing, Mike?
14. Mike: I am washing the dishes.
15. What are Tim and Ben doing?
16. They are watching TV.
17. In the afternoon, my mother is busy. She is washing the dishes. I am making the bed. Helen is sweeping the floor.
18. My father is cleaning the table. Tim and Ben are eating fruit in the living room.
19. Where is Su Hai?
20. Su Hai is in her bedroom. She is reading a storybook. What about Su Yang?
21. Su Yang is in her bedroom too. She is making the bed.

24. The ladybirds go away. Bobby is very angry. The grapes are gone. They are pests!

## 知识点

### 单元主题

Helping our parents 帮助父母

### 核心词汇

- cleaning the car 擦车
- cleaning the table 擦桌子
- sweeping the floor 扫地
- washing the dishes 洗碗
- making the bed 整理床铺
- cooking dinner 做晚饭
- busy 忙碌的
- garden 花园
- sweet 甜的
- grow 生长；种植
- pest 害虫
- ladybird 瓢虫

### 重点句型

- What are you doing?
- I'm cleaning the car.
- What is he/she doing?
- He's/She's sweeping the floor.
- What are they doing?
- They're helping their parents.

### 语法重点

现在进行时：be + 动词 ing，表示某人正在做某事。

- I am cleaning the table.
- He is cooking dinner.
- She is washing the dishes.
- You are making the bed.
- We are cleaning the room.
- They are helping their parents.

### 易错提醒

- 动词加 ing 时注意去 e 和双写：make -> making, write -> writing, sweep -> sweeping, run -> running。
- 主语不同，be 动词不同：I 用 am，he/she 用 is，you/we/they 用 are。
- 问句和答句的人称要对应。

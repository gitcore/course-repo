# 五年级第三单元 - Asking the way

学习问路

## 课程信息

- **难度**: 初级
- **级别**: 1

## Story time

1. Yang Ling wants to visit Su Hai's new home.
2. Yang Ling: How do I get to your home, Su Hai?
3. Su Hai: You can take the metro. You can get on the metro at Park Station and get off at City Library Station. Then, walk to Moon Street. There's a bookshop on the street. My Home is next to it.
4. Yang Ling: All right.
5. Yang Ling comes out of City Library Station. She is on Sun Street. She cannot find the bookshop. She asks a policeman for help.
6. Yang Ling: Excuse me, how do I get to the bookshop on Moon Street?
7. Policeman: Go along this street. Turn right at the traffic lights. Then, go along Moon Street. You can see the bookshop on your right.
8. Yang Ling: Thank you.
